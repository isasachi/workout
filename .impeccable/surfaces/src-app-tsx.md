Scope: the whole app (Hoy, Plan, Progreso, exercise sheet, session mode). Mode: operate.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A coach on the floor beside you: one exercise, one animated figure, one number at a time. Refuses the catalog-of-cards workout app where the beginner must read to understand.
OWN-WORLD: Category standard (Apple Fitness / NTC). True-black ground, #1C1C1E surfaces, white ink, one lime accent for actions and done-state, session-type hues (strength lime, cardio cyan, HIIT pink, recovery amber) only as small markers. Recovery is amber, not blue: a second blue reads as cardio's cyan at dot size, and the blue-violet that would separate them is a house-banned hue. Premium pass (user request 2026-10-25): Archivo Variable as the display voice, wide (112%) and heavy for titles, condensed (82%) for every big numeral; SF Pro for body and labels. Surfaces #161618 / #262629 / #3a3a3e on true black. Soft 20px radii, pill buttons, iOS tab bar. Technique stage: a 3D clay mannequin (three.js) with sculpted limbs, key + cool rim light, soft floor shadow and a studio floor that fades into the background; working muscles in lime. A week strip of day capsules coloured by session sits under the title on Hoy, and a three-number stats strip sits under the stage.
STORY: Opens on today's session, understands it from the figure, taps Empezar, follows step by step, marks sets, sees weeks fill.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Today: day + week line, session title large, duration/intensity line, full-width animated preview of the first exercise, Empezar pill at thumb reach; exercise list below.
FORM: Canon (standing exit), seed: seed:d89e14c0b31640a8 (degraded roll, user took canon). Signature move: the 3D technique figure with the full list of the rep's steps fixed under it; only the highlight moves to the current step, so nothing has to be read in a hurry (user reported cues changing too fast). Timed sessions show one fixed cue per move. Demo controls sit in the stage's top corner, clear of the figure.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
